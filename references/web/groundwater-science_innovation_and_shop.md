## Innovation page: FlowSource entry (paraphrased)
- Entry title: "FlowSource (FS20)".
- Described as a groundwater capture delineation tool for MODFLOW, "compatible with all major versions since 1996 spanning to present MODFLOW6".
- Works in forward and backtracking modes, which indicate fate and provenance respectively.
- Other tools on the same page that may matter for an MF6 workflow:
  - SWAc (recharge/runoff model; free);
  - VKD for MODFLOW 6;
  - the HFR (Head and Flow Rule) package for MF6 (licensed);
  - a GPU-parallel MODFLOW-USG, built with HydroAlgorythmics.
