## Figure 1

**Figure 1.** Full-mixing flow tracing. (a) A fully mixed cell $i$ receives cell-to-cell flows $q_{ji}$ and source terms (here recharge) and passes water to neighbours ($q_{ik}$) and sink terms (here a well, $d_i$). $I_i$ and $O_i$ are its total inflow and outflow; the cell budget error $r_i = I_i - O_i$ is reported, not forced to zero. All outflows carry the same water, so the backward fraction $F_i$ is the share leaving directly through the destination plus the outflow-weighted mean of the downstream fractions. (b) Backward fractions $F$ to a 6 m³/d well on an eight-cell example with boundary inflow (west), recharge of 1 on every cell and a river (east), under gross accounting; numbers on arrows are flows. The well takes 6 of the 11 units passing through its cell, so $F = 6/11 = 0.55$ there and in the cells that drain only through it. <!-- README_method.md Fig. 1; studies/paper/method_figures.py --> (c) Gross and net accounting at the 5.0 m³/d weak-sink well of tutorial 02, whose cell carries 33.7 m³/d. Gross accounting gives $F = 0.15$ in the well cell and credits the well with 5.0 m³/d, its pumping; net accounting, as in the reference method (Foley and Black 2013), fixes $F = 1$ and credits 33.7 m³/d, 6.7 times the pumping. <!-- README_method.md Fig. 1c; tutorials/02_accounting --> (d) Transient models. Left: a snapshot traces one time step as a steady state on its own flows; storage release is a source term, so the water it supplies has no origin, and nothing is carried to the next step. Right: transient tracking carries the cell's water volume $V_i$ and tagged fraction $c_i$ from step to step. The water stored at the end of step $n$ mixes with the inflows of step $n+1$, and the outflows and the water stored at the end of step $n+1$ carry the mixture $c_i^{n+1}$. Storage terms change $V_i$ and are not source or sink terms, so water released from storage is water of known tagged fraction stored in the previous step. Shading shows the tagged fraction. The sketch leaves out the cell budget error, which leaves at $c_i^{n+1}$ (flow-error correction).
