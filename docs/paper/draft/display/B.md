## Figure 1

**Figure 1.** Full-mixing flow tracing. (a) A fully mixed cell $i$ with cell-to-cell flows, recharge (source term) and a well (sink term, $d_i$). These define $I_i$, $O_i$ and $r_i$ (Eq. 1). (b) Backward fractions $F$ (in cells) to a 6 m³/d well in an eight-cell example (gross accounting). Water enters on the west and as recharge. It leaves through the well and a river. Arrow widths show flows. Only the well cell's flows are numbered (m³/d). In the well cell, $F = 6/11 = 0.55$. (c) Water credited to a weak-sink well pumping 5.0 m³/d in a three-layer example model: 5.0 m³/d (gross accounting) or 33.7 m³/d (net accounting; Section 2.3). (d) Left: a snapshot traces one step on that step's flows. Storage release is a source of unknown origin. Right: transient tracking carries a cell's water volume $V_i$ and tagged fraction $c_i$ (shading) from step $n$ to $n+1$ (Section 2.7).
