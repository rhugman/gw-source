## Figure 1

**Figure 1.** Full-mixing flow tracing. (a) A fully mixed cell $i$ with cell-to-cell inflow $q_{ji}$ and outflow $q_{ik}$, a source term (recharge) and a sink term (a well, $d_i$). Its total inflow, total outflow and cell budget error are $I_i$, $O_i$ and $r_i$ (§2.1). (b) Backward fractions $F$ to a well on an eight-cell example under gross accounting, with boundary inflow on the west, recharge on every cell and a river on the east. Numbers on arrows are flows (m³/d); numbers in cells are $F$. (c) Water credited to a weak-sink well under gross and net accounting (§2.3). (d) Transient models. Left: a snapshot traces one time step on its own flows, with storage release as a source term of unknown origin (§2.7). Right: transient tracking follows the same cell through time (§2.8); the water stored at the end of step $n$ mixes with the inflows of step $n+1$, and the outflows and stored water carry the mixture. Shading shows the tagged fraction $c$. The sketch leaves out the cell budget error.
