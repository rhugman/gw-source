## Figure 3

**Figure 3.** Numerical dispersion of full mixing against cell size h. F is the share of a cell's water that ends at the well. Top row: problem A, uniform flow to a well, with the analytical capture zone (Javandel and Tsang 1986) as reference; flow along the grid rows (solid) and at 30° to them (dotted). Bottom row: problem B, an aquifer like model 1, with PRT on the finest grid as reference, compared west of the river. (a, d) Width of the transition band (0.1 < F < 0.9); dashed guide, narrowing as $h^{0.5}$. Face-to-face routing (Chowdhury 2016), an experimental 2D option that routes water between cell faces instead of mixing it, removes 35–70 % of the excess spreading but keeps the rate. The cell-averaged exact zone (A) and PRT on the same grid (B) set a floor of about one cell. (b, e) Relative error in the size of the F ≥ 0.1 area. (c, f) The same for the F ≥ 0.5 area; grey band, ±5 %.
