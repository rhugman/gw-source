**Figure 10.** Capture probability before and after history matching on the shared example model (Section 4.9). The capture probability P(F > 0.5) of a cell is the share of realisations in which more than half of the cell's water ends at the well. (a) P(F > 0.5) over 50 prior realisations of the hydraulic conductivity field. (b) P(F > 0.5) over the same 50 realisations after history matching to heads with PEST++ IES. In (a) and (b), colour gives P(F > 0.5) from 0 (pale yellow) to 1 (dark blue). Black lines: P(F > 0.5) = 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9. Red dashed line: F = 0.5 in the synthetic true model, which supplied the measured heads. White circles: the 40 cells with head measurements. The river and the well are marked. (c) Share of the pumped water that comes from the river, in each prior and posterior realisation. Each dot is one realisation. Boxes span the 25 to 75 % range and whiskers the 5 to 95 % range. Red dashed line: the river share of the true model.
