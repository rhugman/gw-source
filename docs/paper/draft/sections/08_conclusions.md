# 8. Conclusions

We write the reference method of full-mixing flow tracing (Foley and Black 2013; Black and Foley 2013) as one sparse linear system per direction, with gross accounting by default and net accounting as an option. The closure and forward–backward checks are exact identities once the cell budget errors are included; both held on the test models, and the closure check on Neversink. Transient tracking carries tagged water through storage: forward, with GWT's water volumes, it matches the dispersion-free unit-concentration transport of MODFLOW 6 GWT, and backward in time it gives what snapshots cannot, the fractions of each cell's water that reach the destination, leave through other sinks or stay stored. The same code reads every MODFLOW 6 grid type, multi-model simulations and package networks, and steady traces are cheap enough for ensembles.

On Neversink, full mixing lies within the spread of particle results under different conventions on the same flow field, and the particle conventions and the treatment of streams change the contributing area. A protection zone should be reported with its method, its particle conventions and its threshold.
