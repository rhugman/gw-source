# 7. Limitations

Full mixing is grid-dependent. The transition band narrows only as about h^0.5 (§4.3), so a finer grid spreads the edge of a zone over more cells. <!-- DS §2, §4, Summary --> Face-to-face routing removes 35 to 70 % of the excess spreading, but it is 2D, does nothing in cells with weak sinks or vertical through-flow, and remains experimental. <!-- DS Summary, §4, §6 -->

Steady and snapshot results have no travel lag. Transient tracking is advection only, with upstream weighting and implicit time steps, and it is verified and demonstrated on synthetic models only (§4.2, §4.4); Neversink is steady state, so the paper has no real-model transient application. Every time step must be saved (OC BUDGET ALL), and memory is one (steps × cells) array per group and output: about 1.6 GB per array at 1.68 million cells and 121 steps, stored as float64. <!-- PLAN Phase 4B notes (Output); NS §1 (1,683,680 cells); 1,683,680 × 121 × 8 bytes = 1.63e9 --> Transient tracking is timed only on tutorial 08 (§3.3).

The Neversink comparison is on the uppermost active layer only. The scenario rule and the pairing of the published areas with the realisations are inferred. The maxrange scenario, in which each priority well's rate is drawn at random between its base and maximum rate, independently per well and realisation, is reproduced in distribution only. <!-- NS §7, §8 --> Some mechanism statements are inferred rather than traced cell by cell: that stream leakage stays on flow lines to the wells along particle paths (Text S5), and that the published probability follows narrow paths where it departs from mean F (Fig. 8). <!-- NS §6, §7, §8 -->

Two limits concern particle tracking. MF6 PRT aborts in cells wet only by a thin film, so one published start was left out; a reproducer is prepared but not yet reported upstream [check: cite the issue once reported]. <!-- NS §3, §8; studies/neversink/prt_film_repro/ --> And PRT supports DIS and DISV grids only, so on DISU grids the full-mixing mean travel time is the only travel-time option. <!-- PLAN §2.8 -->
