# 7. Limitations

The checks test the tracing, not the flow model: a model with wrong recharge gives wrong areas that pass every check. Full mixing is also grid-dependent. The transition band narrows only as about $h^{0.5}$ (§4.3), so halving the cell size narrows it by about 30 %. <!-- DS §2, §4, §5, Summary; 1 − 0.5^0.5 = 0.29 --> Each cell mixes over its saturated thickness, so thick layers spread an area vertically as well as laterally. Face-to-face routing removes 35 to 70 % of the excess spreading, but it is 2D, does nothing in cells with weak sinks or vertical through-flow, and remains experimental. <!-- DS Summary, §4, §6 -->

Steady and snapshot results have no travel lag. Transient tracking is advection only, with upstream weighting and implicit time steps, and it is verified and demonstrated on synthetic models only (§4.2, §4.4); Neversink is steady state, so the paper has no real-model transient application. Every time step must be saved (OC BUDGET ALL), and memory is one (steps × cells) array per group and output: about 1.6 GB per array at 1.68 million cells and 121 steps. <!-- PLAN Phase 4B notes (Output); NS §1 (1,683,680 cells); 1,683,680 × 121 × 8 bytes = 1.63 × 10⁹ -->

The Neversink comparison is against a benchmark (§5) and on the uppermost active layer only. The scenario rule and the pairing of the published areas with the realisations are inferred. The maxrange scenario is reproduced in distribution only. <!-- NS §7, §8 --> Two mechanism statements are inferred rather than traced cell by cell: that stream leakage stays on flow lines to the wells along particle paths {SI: faster growth of weight-losing particles}, and that the narrow paths the published probability follows are single particle paths (§5.5; Fig. 8). <!-- NS §6, §7, §8 -->

PRT supports DIS and DISV grids only, so on DISU grids the full-mixing mean travel time is the only travel-time option. <!-- PLAN §2.8 --> One published start was left out because MF6 PRT aborts in a cell wet only by a thin film {SI: PRT abort in thin-film cells}. <!-- NS §3, §8 -->
