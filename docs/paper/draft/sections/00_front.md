# Contributing areas and sources of water for any MODFLOW 6 sink, in steady and transient flow

**Article Impact Statement:** Open-source code maps the contributing areas of wells, streams and other MODFLOW 6 sinks in seconds and checks every result against the water budget.

**Abstract:** Protection zones restrict land use over a supply well's contributing area, where water enters the ground and later reaches the well. The area is usually mapped by particle tracking, which follows water particles through a groundwater model. Where a well or stream takes only part of a cell's water, particles need a stop-or-pass rule that changes the area. Full-mixing methods such as FlowSource (Foley and Black 2013; Black and Foley 2013), which is used for protection zones in England, need no such rule because they treat each cell's water as mixed. Our open-source MODFLOW 6 code extends the method. It computes exactly the share of each cell's water that ends at the well, even where water circulates. It credits each well with its pumping, not all the water passing its cell. Every result is checked against the model's water budget.
