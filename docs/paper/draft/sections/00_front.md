# Contributing areas and sources of pumped water from MODFLOW 6 flows, checked against the water budget

**Article Impact Statement:** Open-source contributing areas for MODFLOW 6 wells in seconds, each checked against the water budget, including how much pumped water was already stored.

**Abstract:** Protection zones around supply wells restrict activities on the land whose water reaches the well, its contributing area. That area is usually mapped with a groundwater model. Particle tracking, the usual method, follows water along flow paths. Where a well or stream takes only part of a cell's water, a particle must stop or pass. That choice changes the area. Full-mixing methods, such as MODALL and FlowSource (Foley and Black 2013; Black and Foley 2013), instead treat each cell as fully mixed. They give the share of each cell's water that ends at the well. FlowSource is used for protection zones in England. It is not open source. We extend the method in open-source code for MODFLOW 6. Shares are solved exactly, also where water circulates between cells. Each well is credited only with what it pumps. Every trace is checked against the water budget. The code reads every MODFLOW 6 grid type and boundary package, multi-model simulations and package networks. For transient models, water is tracked through storage, forward and backward in time. Backward tracking tells a manager how much pumped water was already stored in the aquifer.
