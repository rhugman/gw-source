# Full-mixing flow tracing for MODFLOW 6: contributing areas, budget checks and transient tracking

**Article Impact Statement:** Open MODFLOW 6 tool: well contributing areas in seconds, checked against the water budget, and how much pumped water was already stored.

**Abstract:** Contributing areas of supply wells are usually delineated by particle tracking, which needs a stop-or-pass rule at every weak sink. Full-mixing flow tracing treats each cell as fully mixed and computes from the cell-by-cell flows the fraction of each cell's water that leaves through a destination (backward) or came from a source (forward). Its published form (the reference method), used for source protection zones in England, is described only in conference papers and has no open implementation. We write it as one sparse linear system per direction, solved exactly. Two exact identities check every trace against the water budget: traced volumes add up to each destination's or source's inflow, and backward and forward tracing agree. For transient models, tagged water is carried through storage; with the water volumes of the MODFLOW 6 transport model (GWT), forward tracking matches its dispersion-free unit-concentration runs to round-off. <!-- verification_table.md, transient tracking checks: tracking vs GWT ≤ 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ (model 6), 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ (model 7), "gwt" volumes --> New here is backward tracking in time (not particle backward tracking): the fractions of each cell's water, at each time, that leave through the destination or other sinks or stay stored.
