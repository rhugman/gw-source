## {SI: net accounting, fixed cells}

Under net accounting the destination cells are fixed at F = 1 (forward: the source cells at G = 1). Which cells are fixed depends on how the group was selected. A pure cell or zone selection fixes all its cells, as FlowSource defines groups (Foley and Black 2013). A selection with any term criterion (package, boundary name, term), also when combined with cells or a zone, fixes only the cells where a selected term flows in the traced direction: a sink in a backward trace, a source in a forward trace. A cell with selected terms of both signs is fixed in both directions, and a group with no such term fixes no cell, so every fraction is 0. A well listed at zero rate, a drain above the water table, or a losing river cell in a backward trace is therefore not fixed. On a stream network, a package selection fixes only the reaches whose records flow in the traced direction; to fix a whole stream, select its reaches as cells. <!-- PLAN §2.4; src/gwsource/trace.py::net_fixed_cells -->

Net accounting has no cell budget error term. The water it attributes to a group counts each passage once: the throughflow of the fixed cells, less the water passing from one fixed cell to another and less the water that leaves the group and reaches it again. Its closure check (volume from the sources equals this net inflow) tests the sum over the cells outside the group, not each cell: its error is exactly the sum of those cells' equation errors. <!-- PLAN §2.4; src/gwsource/assemble.py and diagnostics.py docstrings -->

## {SI: travel-time identity}

Multiplying each cell's travel-time equation (Eq. 5) by its outflow and adding over all cells gives, with the cell budget errors $r_i$,

$$\sum_i \mathrm{Src}_i\, m_i - \sum_i r_i\, m_i = \sum_i V_i F_i .$$

Divided by the destination inflow $\sum_i d_i$, the right-hand side is the turnover time: the volume of water that ends at the destination divided by the destination's inflow. By the backward closure check (Eq. 3), the left-hand side divided by the same inflow is the flow-weighted mean travel time of all water reaching the destination. The two are therefore equal once the cell budget errors are included. <!-- src/gwsource/traveltime.py docstring, "Exact identity (backward)" -->

The mean travel time $m_i/F_i$ of Section 2.6 includes the residence in the cell where the water leaves, for a well its own cell. The time until the water enters that cell is $(m_i - x_i)/F_i$, where $x$ solves $(\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{P})\,\mathbf{x} = \boldsymbol{\tau} \circ (\mathbf{S}\,\mathbf{D})$, the expected residence in the cell of exit, weighted by the share leaving there through the destination. Particle trackers that stop particles on entering a sink cell (MODFLOW 6 PRT, MODPATH) measure this second time; it is the full-mixing time compared with particles in Section 4.1. <!-- src/gwsource/traveltime.py docstring (mean_time_to_exit_cell) --> On model 1, the water reaching the well takes 9,607 d on average including its time in the well cell, and 9,574 d to enter that cell. <!-- PLAN Phase 3 notes, PRT (model 1: Eulerian 9574 d to the exit cell, 9607 d including it) -->
