## {SI: PRT abort in thin-film cells}

MODFLOW 6 PRT (6.9.0 development build) aborts when a particle is released in, or flows into, a cell wet only by a film so thin that the cell top PRT computes rounds to the cell bottom. In the calibrated Neversink model one published start lies in such a cell (saturation 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁶) and was left out of the PRT runs.
