### The rebuilt model of Bedekar et al. (2024)

We rebuilt the MODFLOW-2005 model of Bedekar et al. (2024, pp. 305–306 and Fig. 1) in MODFLOW 6. The grid has 101 × 101 cells of 10 m and three layers of 10 m, with the top at 30 m. The horizontal hydraulic conductivity is 10 and the vertical conductivity 1. The specific storage is 10⁻⁵ m⁻¹, the specific yield 0.1 and the porosity 0.1. Lateral inflow of 454.5 m³/d enters along the west column of all three layers. Recharge of 3 × 10⁻⁴ m/d falls on layer 1. A stream (RIV) fills the east column, with a stage of 30 m and a conductance of 1,000 m²/d per cell. The well pumps 700 m³/d for 25 years after a steady-state period of 1 day without pumping. The paper leaves some values open. We made the following choices. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table and budget (INFLOW 454.5, RCH 306.0, PUMP 700.0); tutorials/10_unit_concentration/helpers.py (SPEC: 101 × 101 cells of 10 m, top 30, k 10, rech 3e-4, riv_col 100, riv_stage 30, riv_cond 1000, well (50, 85) -700; LAYER_BOTM (20, 10, 0); build_bedekar: kv 1, ss 1e-5, sy 0.1, inflow_rate 1.5) -->

- The paper reports the hydraulic conductivity in feet per day and the other inputs in metric units. We read it as metres per day. A rerun in feet per day moved the long-run shares by at most 0.1 percentage points. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table (assumption) and §5 cell 14 output (steady shares 0.483/0.292/0.225 against 0.482/0.291/0.226) -->
- All three layers are convertible. The river bottom is at the bottom of each cell.
- The well is in layer 2, where Fig. 1 of the paper draws its screen, at row 51, column 86. That is 150 m west of the stream cell centres.
- The pumping period has 300 equal steps of 30.4 days. The tracking uses the "gwt" water volumes.
- All the water in the model at the start is tagged as initial water. The paper instead fixes the concentration of the stored water at 1 during the first period. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table (assumptions: convertible layers, river bottom at the cell bottom, well in layer 2 at row 51, column 86, 25 years in 300 steps of 30.4 days, stored water tagged at the start), settings (POROSITY 0.1) and §2 (TrackingConfig(porosity=POROSITY, initial_water=INITIAL, volume_mode="gwt")) -->
