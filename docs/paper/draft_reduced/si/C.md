### Verification

The automated tests run on ten small test models and two hand-built networks. {SI: verification checks on the test models} shows the largest error of each check. The test models cover the DIS, DISV and DISU grids, the XT3D option, storage, streams (SFR), the water mover (MVR), multi-aquifer wells (MAW), lakes (LAK), the unsaturated zone (UZF) and simulations split into linked models. Test model 1 is the base case. It is one layer of 20 × 30 cells of 50 m, with constant heads on the west edge, a river along one column, recharge and a well pumping 800 m³/d. The two hand-built networks are the worked example of Foley and Black (2013) and a network of 15 cells with flow loops. The archived code release (Section 7) describes the test models in tests/models/README.md. ### The example model

The example model is one convertible layer (DIS) of 40 × 40 cells of 50 m, 2 km on each side. The top is at 20 m and the bottom at −30 m. The hydraulic conductivity is 5 m/d everywhere. Constant heads of 10 m (CHD) fill the 40 cells of column 1. A river (RIV) fills the 40 cells of column 31, with a stage of 6 m, a bottom of 4 m and a conductance of 500 m²/d per cell. Recharge of 5 × 10⁻⁴ m/d (RCH) falls on every cell. The supply well (WEL) pumps 800 m³/d from row 21, column 29. Its cell centre is 100 m west of the river cell centres. The model is steady state. Travel times and particle tracking use a porosity of 0.3. <!-- tutorials/herebedragons.py (AquiferSpec: nrow = ncol = 40, delr = delc = 50, top 20, botm -30, k 5, strt 10, rech 5e-4, chd_head 10, riv_col 30 (0-based), riv_stage 6, riv_cond 500, riv_rbot 4, wells supply_well (20, 28) -800; build_quickstart: icelltype 1); tutorials/06_travel_time/06_travel_time.ipynb settings (POROSITY 0.3) -->

### The ensemble and history matching

The 50 realisations of the ensemble differ only in their hydraulic conductivity. Each field is set at 100 pilot points, 200 m apart, and interpolated to every cell by simple kriging of $\ln K$ around $\ln 5$. The correlation between two points a distance $r$ apart is $\exp(-(r/400\ \mathrm{m})^2)$. The prior draws the pilot-point values as $K = 5 \exp(0.8\, z)$, where $z$ is a correlated standard normal variable. <!-- tutorials/09_ensembles/09_ensembles.ipynb settings, cell 2 (N_REAL 50, PRIOR_SEED 3, SIGMA_LNK 0.8, CORRELATION_LENGTH 400.0, PP_SPACING 4, NUGGET 1e-4) and §1, cells 3 and 4 (simple kriging of ln K around ln 5); tutorials/09_ensembles/helpers.py (pilot_point_cells, gaussian_correlation, kriging_weights, draw_prior) -->

The true aquifer is a random field with the same statistics. It is made by smoothing white noise, not from pilot points. A kriged field is smoother between the pilot points than the true field. So no realisation can reproduce the true field exactly. The river supplies 9.4 % of the water the true model pumps. Its steady-state heads in 40 random cells are the measurements. Each head has added noise with a standard deviation of 0.05 m. PEST++ IES adjusts the 100 pilot-point values of each realisation to fit these heads, with weights of one over the noise. After three iterations, the mean weighted sum of squared head residuals had fallen from 3,491.7 to 55.5. A fit within the noise would give about 40, the number of measurements. The 50 posterior realisations are then rerun and traced in the same way as the prior. <!-- tutorials/09_ensembles/09_ensembles.ipynb settings, cell 2 (TRUTH_SEED 2024, N_OBS 40, OBS_SEED 11, NOISE_SD 0.05, NOISE_SEED 12), §9.1 cells 21 and 22 ("truth: area F > 0.5 = 1.025 km2, river share = 0.094"), §9.2 (log-transformed parameters, weights 1/sigma), cell 24 (noptmax 3), cell 26 phi table (mean 3,491.7 prior, 55.5 posterior) and cell 28 ("reran and traced 50 posterior realisations"); phi table and prior statistics moved to the notebook in SI round 1 -->

### The rebuilt model of Bedekar et al. (2024)

We rebuilt the MODFLOW-2005 model of Bedekar et al. (2024, pp. 305–306 and Fig. 1) in MODFLOW 6. The grid has 101 × 101 cells of 10 m and three layers of 10 m, with the top at 30 m. The horizontal hydraulic conductivity is 10 and the vertical conductivity 1. The specific storage is 10⁻⁵ m⁻¹, the specific yield 0.1 and the porosity 0.1. Lateral inflow of 454.5 m³/d enters along the west column of all three layers. Recharge of 3 × 10⁻⁴ m/d falls on layer 1. A stream (RIV) fills the east column, with a stage of 30 m and a conductance of 1,000 m²/d per cell. The well pumps 700 m³/d for 25 years after a steady-state period of 1 day without pumping. The paper leaves some values open. We made the following choices. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table and budget (INFLOW 454.5, RCH 306.0, PUMP 700.0); tutorials/10_unit_concentration/helpers.py (SPEC: 101 × 101 cells of 10 m, top 30, k 10, rech 3e-4, riv_col 100, riv_stage 30, riv_cond 1000, well (50, 85) -700; LAYER_BOTM (20, 10, 0); build_bedekar: kv 1, ss 1e-5, sy 0.1, inflow_rate 1.5) -->

- The paper gives the hydraulic conductivity in feet per day and the other inputs in metric units. We read it as metres per day. A rerun in feet per day moved the long-run shares by at most 0.1 percentage points. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table (assumption) and §5 cell 14 output (steady shares 0.483/0.292/0.225 against 0.482/0.291/0.226) -->
- All three layers are convertible. The river bottom is at the bottom of each cell.
- The well is in layer 2, where Fig. 1 of the paper draws its screen, at row 51, column 86. That is 150 m west of the stream cell centres.
- The pumping period has 300 equal steps of 30.4 days. The tracking uses the "gwt" water volumes.
- All the water in the model at the start is tagged as initial water. The paper instead fixes the concentration of the stored water at 1 during the first period. <!-- tutorials/10_unit_concentration/10_unit_concentration.ipynb §1 table (assumptions: convertible layers, river bottom at the cell bottom, well in layer 2 at row 51, column 86, 25 years in 300 steps of 30.4 days, stored water tagged at the start), settings (POROSITY 0.1) and §2 (TrackingConfig(porosity=POROSITY, initial_water=INITIAL, volume_mode="gwt")) -->
