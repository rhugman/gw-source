## Figure 1

**Figure 1.** (a) Backward fractions F (in cells) to a 6 m³/d well in an eight-cell example (gross accounting). Water enters from the west and as recharge of 1 m³/d per cell. It leaves through the well and a river. Arrow widths show flows. Only the well cell's flows are numbered (m³/d). In the well cell, F = 6/11 = 0.55. (b) One cell i of a transient model. A snapshot (top) traces each time step on its own flows. Water released from storage then enters the cell as a source of unknown origin. Transient tracking (bottom) carries the water stored in the cell at the end of step n into step n + 1. There it mixes with the inflows of that step. V is the cell's water volume and c the share of its water that is tagged.
