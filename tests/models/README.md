# Test models

The automated tests run on ten small MODFLOW 6 test models and a hand-built network. Models 1 to 10 are built with FloPy by the functions in this folder (`build_model(key, ...)` in `__init__.py`). Each writes the binary grid file and a budget file with SAVE_FLOWS on every package (`common.py`). All cells are 50 m wide in plan, except on the Voronoi grid of model 3. The hand-built network is not a MODFLOW 6 model.

| model | key or file | grid and size | packages | flow | what it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `m1_dis` | DIS, 1 layer of 20 × 30 cells, top 20 m, bottom −30 m; 6 inactive cells in one corner (594 active) | CHD on the west edge (5 m); RIV along one column (stage 6 m); WEL (800 m³/d); RCH (1 × 10⁻³ m/d); K 10 m/d | steady state | the base case: closure, forward–backward and boundary-control checks, GWT at equilibrium, PRT, split into three models; the inactive cells test the mapping of cell numbers |
| 2 | `m2_dis3lay`, `m2_dis3lay_top` | DIS, 3 layers of 15 × 25 cells (1,125 cells), top 10 m, layer bottoms −10, −30 and −60 m | CHD on the west (9 m) and east (2 m) edges of every layer; RCH (5 × 10⁻⁴ m/d) on layer 1; two WEL in layer 3 (400 and 5 m³/d); K 10, 2 and 15 m/d, vertical K one tenth of it | steady state | a weak-sink well (5 m³/d) under gross accounting, GWT at equilibrium, PRT; a copy with recharge on the top cell face (IFLOWFACE = −1) tests PRT release on a face |
| 3 | `m3_disv` | DISV Voronoi grid over model 1's area, from a Triangle mesh with triangles of at most 2,500 m²; all cells active | as model 1, placed by geometry; river conductance scaled to model 1's total | steady state | reading a vertex grid, PRT on a vertex grid, split into two models |
| 4 | `m4_disu_vert`, `m4_disu_novert` | DISU, a cell-for-cell copy of model 1, with and without cell vertices | as model 1 | steady state | DISU results equal DIS results exactly |
| 5 | `m5_xt3d` | as model 1 | as model 1, with XT3D; log-normal K (median 10 m/d), K across the main axis 0.05 times K, main axes rotated by up to 45° | steady state | flow loops |
| 6 | `m6_transient` | as model 1 | as model 1, with STO (specific yield 0.2, specific storage 10⁻⁵ /m); a 1-day steady-state period, then three 100-day periods of four steps with a time-step multiplier of 1.2 (13 steps); pumping 800, 1,200, 400 and 1,000 m³/d; recharge 1, 0.3, 1.5 and 0 times model 1's | transient | 13 snapshots with storage as source and sink terms; transient tracking against GWT; transient forward–backward check; water balances; split into four models |
| 7 | `m7_mover` | as model 1 | model 1 with the river replaced by an SFR stream of 20 reaches (inflow 500 m³/d at the first reach); two DRN drains feeding the first reach through MVR (70 % and 100 % of their outflow); named wells whose list changes between two periods | steady state (two periods) | a package network (SFR and MVR); GWT at equilibrium and transient tracking against GWT with stream and mover transport (SFT and MVT) |
| 8 | `m8_maw` | as model 2 | model 2's grid, with the 400 m³/d well replaced by a MAW well (30 m³/d) screened in all three layers; the 5 m³/d WEL kept; vertical K one hundredth of K | steady state | a multi-aquifer well that moves water between layers |
| 9 | `m9_lak_uzf` | as model 2, with the layer-1 cells of two lakes removed | LAK with two lakes (lake A spills into lake B, lake B out of the model); UZF on a block of layer-1 cells, whose rejected infiltration MVR moves into lake A; RCH (4 × 10⁻⁴ m/d) elsewhere; WEL (150 m³/d) in layer 3; CHD on the west (9 m) and east (4 m) edges; STO | transient: a 1-day steady-state period, then a 50-day period of two steps | a lake and unsaturated-zone network; transient forward–backward check; water balances |
| 10 | `m10_constant` | as model 1 | as model 1, without STO; a 1-day period, then 4 × 10⁵ d in 120 equal steps of unchanged flows | steady-state flows over many time steps | transient tracking reaches the steady-state fractions |
| worked example | `tests/test_fb13_example.py` | hand-built network of 3 × 3 cells and two outside nodes [FB13] | none (flows given between cells) | steady state | net accounting against the published values |

[FB13] is Foley & Black (2013). The tests that use these models are `tests/test_models_run.py`, `test_closure_duality.py`, `test_fb13_example.py`, `test_gwt_equilibrium.py`, `test_prt.py`, `test_multimodel.py` and `test_tracking_mf6.py`. Models 1, 3 and 6 are also split into three, two and four linked models (`tests/test_multimodel.py`, `labels_for`). GWT runs for the comparisons are built by `gwt.py` (`build_gwt_fmi`), with stream and mover transport (SFT and MVT) on model 7.
